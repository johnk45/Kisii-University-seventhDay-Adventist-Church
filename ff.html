OUR MISSION

Sowing First Fruits for Lasting Harvest

First Fruit Ministry at KSUSDA Church inspires believers to dedicate the first portion of their increase — whether financial, time, or resources — back to God. Through teaching, prayer, and practical stewardship, we cultivate a lifestyle of honor and generosity.

We believe the principle of first fruit unlocks provision, breakthroughs, and supernatural multiplication in every area of life. Join our community of faithful sowers.
